## Why Language Is the Biggest Invisible Barrier in Education

A typical Indian student grows up in a multilingual environment:

- **Home language** → Hindi / Tamil / Bengali / Marathi / etc.
- **School language** → Often English
- **Internet language** → Mixed or Hinglish

When complex subjects like Maths and Science are taught in a non-native language, students face two challenges at the same time:

1. **Understanding the concept**
2. **Translating the language**

This double cognitive load slows learning and reduces confidence. Many students know the answer but hesitate to ask the question.

### The Real Impact of Language Barriers

Consider this: A student in Cuttack understands fractions perfectly when explained in Odia by their grandmother using rice distribution examples. But when the same concept is taught in English at school using abstract terminology, confusion sets in.

The student isn't struggling with fractions. They're struggling with English.

This happens to millions of students across India in mathematics, science, and every subject that uses technical vocabulary.
